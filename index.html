About

I am a PhD student in Statistics at Lund University, where I recently started my doctoral studies. My research interests broadly lie in statistics and machine learning, with a particular focus on statistical theory and methodology.

I obtained both my Master's and Bachelor's degrees in Mathematics from the Technical University of Munich (TUM) in 2026. Before starting my PhD, I worked as a research assistant at the Chair of Mathematical Statistics at TUM, where I studied non-Gaussian causal models with latent confounding under the supervision of Prof. Mathias Drton. In addition to my academic experience, I have interned at Deloitte and Munich Re, working on generative AI and data analytics.

Outside academia, I enjoy exploring restaurants, cooking, and having fika with interesting conversations in the afternoon.

I welcome discussions about research ideas and potential collaborations. Please feel free to get in touch.
