About

I am a PhD student in Statistics at Lund University. My research interests are in modern statistics and machine learning, with a focus on statistical theory and methodology. I obtained my Master's and Bachelor's degrees in Mathematics from the Technical University of Munich. Before starting my PhD, I was a research assistant at the Chair of Mathematical Statistics at TUM, where I worked on non-Gaussian causal models with latent confounding under the supervision of Prof. Mathias Drton. I have also interned at Deloitte and Munich Re, working on GenAI and data analytics.
